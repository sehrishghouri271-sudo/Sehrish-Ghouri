ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Sehrish Ghouri

This is the beginning of my official writing era.

I write Urdu novels, afsane, blogs, and more—making my work easily accessible to readers in PDF form. My first novel is currently being released episodically, marking the very first step in my journey as a writer.

For me, writing is an interesting hobby, a way to create stories, explore ideas, and put feelings into words. I have always enjoyed reading books, but I especially love talking about them—discussing the stories, characters, emotions, and everything that makes a book memorable.

Everything I write is very close to my heart. I am only at the beginning of my writing journey, and I know there will be mistakes. You may find places where my writing feels weak or where I still have a lot to learn. But that is something I am willing to embrace. I am, and will always be, learning and trying my best to improve.

This website is a place to share that journey—from my stories and writings to the books I read and the thoughts they leave behind.

This is only the beginning.
